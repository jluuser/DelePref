Figure 2. From evolutionary deletion evidence to structural consequences. (A) Actual local MMseqs2 alignment of the Sec6 CATH domain 2fji101 (PDB 2FJI, author chain 1) to the retrieved fission-yeast Sec6 homolog UniRef50_O74846. WT positions 111-138 contain two six-residue internal gaps; triangles identify the retained WT flanking anchors. The single informative homolog is shown without adding synthetic alignment rows. Homolog-relative absence does not establish the ancestral indel direction or WT deletion tolerance. (B) Frozen scorer-selected Base and homology-guided masks on the same 209-residue WT axis. Both remove exactly 42 residues. The guided plan combines 12 seed residues (orange) with 30 Policy-selected additions (teal), producing a 167-residue sequence. (C) Deposited full-atom WT ribbon and archived predicted shortened structures, shown with shared global cameras and scale. The WT highlights sites removed by the guided plan. Predictions are genuine Cα traces, with a thin gray WT Cα reference; no N/C/O atoms, sidechains or secondary structures were reconstructed. Lower views show the same input-defined event/anchor contact neighborhood under a shared local camera rotated 90 degrees from the global view. WT markers locate residues 42/45 and anchor 134. Colored solid/dashed pair lines denote recovered/unrecovered native contacts; thin gray lines denote WT references. Native contacts 42-134 and 45-134 are separated by 27.6/32.6 Å in Base and 6.7/6.4 Å in the guided prediction. Indices refer to the frozen WT construct, corresponding to PDB author residues 407-615, not the full canonical Sec6 protein. (D) Contact fates use all 536 WT Cα pairs below 8 Å with WT sequence separation at least three, and the explicit frozen WT-to-edit correspondence. Deleted-endpoint, retained-unrecovered and recovered counts are 137/139/260 for Base and 150/58/328 for the guided plan. The net gain of 68 recovered pairs coexists with greater direct contact removal. These fixed-identity counts are distinct from structure-realigned contact scores. This illustrative whole-plan comparison does not establish retained Sec6 function; cohort results and matched-seed completion controls are reported separately.
